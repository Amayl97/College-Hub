College Survival Guide

They don't tell you this.

1. How to manage study?

  • ✔ Take all the classes and pay attention.(This is the first step).
  • ✔ Revise what was thought in the class at home on the same day.
  • ✔ Always ask questions.
  • ✔ If you are scared to ask questions in front of whole class, sit in front row, it will make you think like you are the only one in class. It really helps.
  • ✔ Romanticize study. Drink coffee, smell nice, listen to music while studying.
  • ✔ Don't leave everything for weekend.
  • ✔ Stay organized. Keep your notes, other study material organized, even in the laptop keep them organized. You can make separate folder for each semester and for subjects. Messy data wastes a lot of time.

2. How to be a topper?

  • ✔ If you are in your first semester it is pretty easy for you to be a topper.
  • ✔ Always do your work on time, it's an affiliated college here things are different not difficult.
  • ✔ Always be prepared. Prepare for exams in advance, don't wait for the last day to study.
  • ✔ Don't worry much about the sessional marks. Least sessional marks given are 10 to 13 out of 15 in our college. so you don't have to worry about these.
  • ✔ But prepare class quiz well, this prepares you for the final and mid term exams
  • ✔ Teach others in class, it will clear your doubts too.
  • ✔ Marks distribution:
  • there are total 100 marks for each subject regardless of credit hour.
  • ✨ Mids : 25 marks(these are in the hands of college teacher)
  • ✨ Sessional: 15 marks(also college teacher)
  • ✨ Final exam: 60 marks

3. What kind of relation you should have with teachers?

  • ✔ Teachers here are really cooperative but...
  • ✔ Don't mess with their rules. It is more strict than a school
  • ✔ You can ask questions, request for anything but never argue with them
  • ✔ If you are from IT department. You must be careful with your HOD.
  • ✔ HOD of IT department has a weird habit of asking questions from previous lecture. If you study from slides he will ask from book but if you study book he will ask from slides. So be careful and study from both.
  • ✔ Never give him any chance to insult you, because once this starts no one can end it. So always be prepared.
  • ✔ His mid term exams are the most difficult to pass. So prepare well.Check here.
  • ✔ He will stress you out on mid terms. So try your best to pass his exams. And never ever argue with him.
  • ✔ He is the most difficult one. So just do as he say. You will be at peace.

4. How to manage attendance?

  • ✔ We don't have a concept of 75% attendence. It entirely depends on the HOD of department.
  • ✔ For IT department it is like schools, you have to write application and then you can take leave otherwise your attendance needs to be 100%.
  • ✔ If your application is not accepted and you don't attend the class, you will be fined.
  • ✔ So just make sure you are not from IT department 😭(Just kidding btw)
  • ✔ So basically we have 100% attendance rule.

5. Connection with classmates and college fellows

  • ✔ There is no concept of ragging or bullying in our college.
  • ✔ So be nice to everyone.
  • ✔ And keep in mind that not everyone is your friend.
  • ✔ But make atleast one real friend in your class because in final year you have to make a final year project. If you don't make friends it will be tough for you to find a partner.
